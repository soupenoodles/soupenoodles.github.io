Privacy Policy

Samurai Ninja Evolution · by Soupe Noodles · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game Samurai Ninja Evolution ("the game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by Soupe Noodles ("we", "us"). It is currently available to play in web browsers on CrazyGames, and an Android version is coming soon to Google Play. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. There is no in-game chat, and players cannot send messages, images or other content to each other.

2. Your ninja name

Other players see a display name above your ninja. If you are logged in to CrazyGames, the game uses your CrazyGames username (CrazyGames requires this for multiplayer games). Otherwise you get a random ninja name (for example "SwiftFox42"), which you can change in the menu. Names typed in the game go through a word filter, and names shown from other players are filtered too.

3. Your progress

Your progress (for example damage, trophies, pets, swords, auras, rebirths, tutorial and event rewards) and your settings (volume, graphics, mute, name) are saved:

  • on your device, in your browser's storage for the game, and
  • with CrazyGames, using the CrazyGames cloud-save service, when you play on CrazyGames. This keeps your progress if you play on another device while logged in to your CrazyGames account.

We (Soupe Noodles) do not run our own servers and do not receive or store your save data. If you clear your browser data while not logged in to CrazyGames, your progress may be lost.

4. Online multiplayer

When you are online, you can see other players walking around the same world. To make this work the game connects to Photon (Exit Games), a multiplayer service. While you are connected, the following is shared with the other players in your room (up to 16) through Photon's servers:

  • your display name and rank (number of rebirths),
  • your ninja's position, movement and attacks,
  • the sword, aura and pets your ninja is using, and whether you are inside a battle zone.

This information is only used live, to show your ninja to others, and is gone when you leave. Like any internet service, Photon processes your IP address to connect you to its servers. Training, fights, rewards and saves are not sent through Photon. You can play offline: if the connection fails or you choose to stay offline, nothing is shared with other players.

6. Advertising

On CrazyGames, ads are provided and managed by CrazyGames and its advertising partners. Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for a boost. Ad personalization and consent are handled by CrazyGames according to your region and choices. See the CrazyGames privacy policy for details.

7. Android version (coming soon)

An Android version is coming soon to Google Play. Before it is released, we will update this policy to describe any additional services it uses (for example for ads or app analytics) and the choices you will have.

8. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and show other players online.
  • To keep names appropriate (word filter).
  • To show optional rewarded ads that support the game.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not try to identify individual players.

9. Sharing

We do not share information with anyone except the services listed above, which process it to run the game or under their own terms, and where required by law.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: deleted when you clear your browser's site data for the game.
  • CrazyGames cloud save and account: managed by CrazyGames; you can manage or delete your CrazyGames account in their settings or contact them.
  • Multiplayer data: only exists while you are connected.

If you contact us by email or through our website form, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

11. Children's privacy

The game is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game has no chat or way for players to share personal information with each other. Account, age and consent settings on CrazyGames are handled by CrazyGames. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We keep what we handle to a minimum and rely on the security of CrazyGames and Photon. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise a right, contact us using the details below. For data held by CrazyGames or Photon, you can also contact them directly.
