Privacy Policy

Mine and Steal an Egg · by Soupe Noodles · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game Mine and Steal an Egg ("the game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by Soupe Noodles ("we", "us"). It is available on Android through Google Play and in web browsers on CrazyGames. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. There is no account, no in-game chat, and players cannot send messages, images or other content to each other.

2. Your progress

Your progress (for example cash, eggs, pickaxes, pets, trails, upgrades, daily rewards and quests) and your settings (such as volume) are saved on your device, in your device's app storage (Android) or your browser's storage for the game (web).

We (Soupe Noodles) do not run our own servers and do not receive or store your save data. If you clear the app's data, uninstall the game or clear your browser data, your progress may be lost.

4. Advertising

On Android, ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers to show ads and measure them. In regions where consent is required (for example the EU/EEA and the UK) the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See how Google uses data from ads.

On the web, ads are provided and managed by CrazyGames and its advertising partners. Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for a boost. Ad personalization and consent are handled by CrazyGames according to your region and choices. See the CrazyGames privacy policy for details.

5. Google Play

The Android version is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect technical and usage data (for example installs, crashes and ratings) under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from Google Play.

6. How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To show ads, including optional rewarded ads, that support the game.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not try to identify individual players.

7. Sharing

We do not share information with anyone except the services listed above, which process it to show ads or run the game, under their own terms, and where required by law.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: deleted when you uninstall the game, clear its app data, or clear your browser's site data.
  • Ad data (AdMob): held by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • CrazyGames data and account: managed by CrazyGames; you can manage or delete your CrazyGames account in their settings or contact them.

If you contact us by email, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

9. Children's privacy

The game is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game has no chat or way for players to share personal information with each other. Account, age and consent settings on CrazyGames are handled by CrazyGames. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We keep what we handle to a minimum and rely on the security of Google and CrazyGames. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise a right, contact us using the details below. For data held by Google or CrazyGames, you can also contact them directly.
