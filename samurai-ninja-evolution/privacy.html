Privacy Policy

Samurai Ninja Evolution · by Soupe Noodles · Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game Samurai Ninja Evolution ("the game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by Soupe Noodles ("we", "us"). It is available to play in web browsers on CrazyGames and as an Android app on Google Play. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. There is no in-game chat, and players cannot send messages, images or other content to each other. The game does not ask you to create an account.

2. Your ninja name

Other players see a display name above your ninja. If you are logged in to CrazyGames, the game uses your CrazyGames username (CrazyGames requires this for multiplayer games). Otherwise you get a random ninja name (for example "SwiftFox42"), which you can change in the menu. Names typed in the game go through a word filter, and names shown from other players are filtered too.

3. Your progress

Your progress (for example damage, trophies, pets, swords, auras, rebirths, tutorial and event rewards) and your settings (volume, graphics, mute, name) are saved:

  • on your device, in the game's local storage (browser storage on the web, app storage on Android), and
  • with CrazyGames, using the CrazyGames cloud-save service, when you play on CrazyGames. This keeps your progress if you play on another device while logged in to your CrazyGames account.

On Android, your save stays on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your progress is lost. We do not store your full save on our servers; see section 5 for the small amount of progress information the game sends to our backend.

4. Online multiplayer

When you are online, you can see other players walking around the same world. To make this work the game connects to Photon (Exit Games), a multiplayer service. While you are connected, the following is shared with the other players in your room (up to 16) through Photon's servers:

  • your display name and rank (number of rebirths),
  • your ninja's position, movement and attacks,
  • the sword, aura and pets your ninja is using, and whether you are inside a battle zone.

This information is only used live, to show your ninja to others, and is gone when you leave. Like any internet service, Photon processes your IP address to connect you to its servers. Training, fights, rewards and saves are not sent through Photon. The game also works offline: if the connection fails, nothing is shared with other players.

6. Advertising

Web (CrazyGames). Ads are provided and managed by CrazyGames and its advertising partners. Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for a boost. Ad personalization and consent are handled by CrazyGames according to your region and choices. See the CrazyGames privacy policy for details.

Android (Google AdMob). The Android game shows banner ads, interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown at natural breaks, never in the middle of a fight, and not during your first minutes of play) and optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and other data to show ads and measure them. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where required, the game shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you play, and you can review or change your choice later from Settings → Ad privacy in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). See How Google uses data in ads.

7. Android version

The Android app uses the services marked "Android" in section 5. It does not need access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. It uses the internet connection for multiplayer, ads and crash reports. It is intended for players aged 13 and over (see section 11).

8. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and show other players online.
  • To keep names appropriate (word filter).
  • To find and fix crashes and tune the game (Crashlytics, Remote Config).
  • To post milestone announcements to our Discord community.
  • To show ads (including optional rewarded ads) that support the game.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not try to identify individual players.

9. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the services listed in section 5, which process it to run the game, show ads or fix crashes, and where required by law. Your display name and in-game rank are visible to other players in your room, and the milestone information described in section 5 is visible to members of our Discord server. Ad partners reached through AdMob may receive the data described in section 6 under Google's policies.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall the app (Android), or clear your browser's site data (web).
  • CrazyGames cloud save and account: managed by CrazyGames; you can manage or delete your CrazyGames account in their settings or contact them.
  • Multiplayer data: only exists while you are connected.
  • Unity Gaming Services (anonymous player ID and related data): kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, email us at the address in section 16 with your ninja name; we will delete the data we hold for you, and the associated Unity data, and remove you from future announcements. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete it.
  • Discord announcements: messages already posted on our Discord server can be removed on request.
  • Crash reports and ad data: held by Google (Firebase and AdMob) under their retention policies. Crashlytics data is typically kept for 90 days.

If you contact us by email or through our website form, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

11. Children's privacy

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the game has no chat or way for players to share personal information with each other. Account, age and consent settings on CrazyGames are handled by CrazyGames. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We keep what we handle to a minimum and rely on the security of CrazyGames, Photon, Google and Unity. Data sent to Google and Unity services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise a right, contact us using the details below. For data held by CrazyGames, Photon, Google or Unity, you can also contact them directly. EEA and UK residents can also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.
