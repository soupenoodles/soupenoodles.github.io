Privacy Policy

Wood Chopper by Soupe Noodles · Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information Wood Chopper ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it to what the game needs to work.

1. Who we are

The game is made by Soupe Noodles, an independent developer based in Morocco. Contact: contact@soupenoodles.com or support@soupenoodles.com.

2. Information the game uses

  • Player name. The name you choose (or your CrazyGames username when you are logged in to CrazyGames). It is checked by a word filter and shown to other players in the same room and in the game.
  • Game progress. Level, strength, cash, wood, items, pets, auras, upgrades, rebirths, settings and tutorial progress. It is saved on your device, in Unity Cloud Save under an anonymous ID created by the game, and, on CrazyGames, in your CrazyGames account so progress follows you.
  • Chat messages. Messages you type in the in-game chat are sent in real time to the other players in your room. We do not build profiles from chat. Chat can be switched off in the CrazyGames settings.
  • Multiplayer data. To show other players, the game sends your position, appearance, name and room to Photon (Exit Games), which also receives technical data such as your IP address to run the connection.
  • Gameplay alerts. When a player reaches certain milestones (for example a rare find or a very high total), the server may post a short alert with the player name and the milestone to a private channel that we use to watch for cheating and to run the community. It does not contain your email or other personal details.
  • Technical data. Basic information such as device type, browser and errors may be processed by the platforms below to run and protect the service.

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, location or payment details, and we do not sell personal information.

3. Third parties

  • CrazyGames hosts the web version, shows ads and provides accounts and cloud saves. Their handling of your data is described in the CrazyGames privacy policy.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code) stores your anonymous ID and save data and checks rewards.
  • Photon (Exit Games) runs the multiplayer connection.
  • Discord receives the gameplay alerts described above.

These services have their own privacy policies and may process data in other countries.

4. Ads

Ads in the web version are shown by CrazyGames, only through their system. Optional rewarded videos give in-game bonuses. We do not run our own ad tracking in the web version.

5. Cookies and local storage

The game and the platforms use browser storage (such as local storage and cookies) to keep your save, settings and sign-in. You can clear them in your browser; local progress will then be lost unless it is saved to your CrazyGames account.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep progress for as long as the game is online or until you ask us to delete it. You can use Reset progress in the settings to wipe your save. To delete your data, email support@soupenoodles.com with your player name and we will remove it where we are able to identify it.

7. Children

The game is meant for a general audience aged 12 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Parents should supervise online chat.

8. Security

We use reputable services and basic safeguards, but no online service is perfectly secure. Do not share personal information in chat or in your player name.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Contact us at the addresses above.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
