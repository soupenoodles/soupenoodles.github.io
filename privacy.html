This policy covers only this website, soupenoodles.com, run by Soupe Noodles ("we", "us"), an independent game studio from Morocco. Our games are covered by their own policies, linked above.

1. What you can browse without giving us anything

You can read the whole website without an account. The website has no login, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not set cookies of its own.

2. The contact form

If you send us a message with the contact form, we receive the name, email address, topic and message you type. The form is delivered to our email by Web3Forms, a form-to-email service, which processes the message (and, like any web service, your IP address) only to deliver it and block spam.

We use your message only to answer you. We keep it in our email for as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. You can also email us directly instead of using the form.

3. Services that load with the website

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Google Fonts provides the font used on the home page. When the page loads, your browser requests the font from Google, which receives your IP address and browser information.

These services process this information under their own privacy policies (linked below). We do not receive it.

4. Links to other sites

The website links to other services, such as CrazyGames, Google Play and our Discord server. When you follow a link, that service's own privacy policy applies.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone except the services above that run the website, and where required by law.

6. Children

The website is meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through the contact form, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to how it is used. To do so, contact us using the details below.
