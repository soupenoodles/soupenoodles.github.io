Privacy Policy

La Patate Merge · by Soupe Noodles · Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information La Patate Merge ("the game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by Soupe Noodles ("we", "us"). It is available to play in web browsers on CrazyGames and as an Android app on Google Play.

This policy covers the game on both platforms. CrazyGames, Google and other service providers may also process information under their own privacy policies.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask you to provide your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The game does not include online multiplayer, in-game chat, player messaging, user-created public profiles or leaderboards. You do not need to create an account with Soupe Noodles to play.

If you contact us for support, we receive the email address and message you choose to send us. Please do not include sensitive information in support messages.

2. Your game progress

The game may store gameplay progress and settings so that your game can continue between sessions. Information stored locally may include your current progress, scores, unlocked content and game preferences, depending on the features in the version you play.

  • Web: game data may be stored in your browser's local storage or other browser storage used by the game. Clearing your browser site data may delete this progress.
  • Android: game data may be stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may delete locally stored progress.
  • CrazyGames: CrazyGames may provide platform storage or cloud-save features if these are enabled and used by the game. Any information handled by those features is subject to CrazyGames' privacy policy.

We do not operate a player account system or multiplayer service for this game. Firebase may be used for the technical and app services described in section 4; this does not mean that we maintain a player profile with your real-world identity.

CrazyGames may process information needed to deliver, measure and prevent fraud in connection with ads. Ad personalization and consent choices are handled by CrazyGames according to your region and its platform settings. Please see the CrazyGames Privacy Policy for details.

AdMob and its advertising partners may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, device and app information, and diagnostic information to provide and measure advertising and help prevent fraud. Where required, advertising consent choices are presented through Google's consent tools. You can also review advertising controls in your Android device settings. The available controls vary by device and Android version. See How Google uses data in ads and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Android version

The Android version uses the services marked "Android" in section 3. The game does not require access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location for its gameplay. It uses an internet connection for advertising and the Firebase functionality enabled in the app. Your device and Android settings may provide additional controls over advertising identifiers and app permissions.

6. How we use information

  • To run the game and provide its gameplay features.
  • To save game progress and settings where supported.
  • To understand technical performance and identify or fix crashes and bugs.
  • To configure or improve the game where Firebase remote configuration or analytics features are enabled.
  • To display and measure advertisements and help prevent advertising fraud.
  • To respond when you contact us for support or privacy requests.
  • To protect the game and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not try to identify individual players from the game data we receive.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the services listed in section 3 so they can host the game, provide platform features, display and measure ads, or provide Firebase functionality. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of users, the game or our services.

We do not operate multiplayer for La Patate Merge, so the game does not share your live player movements or gameplay with other players.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Browser storage: locally stored game progress is generally removed when you clear the game's site data or browser storage.
  • Android device storage: locally stored game progress is generally removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • CrazyGames: information held by CrazyGames, including any platform or cloud-save data, is managed under CrazyGames' policies and account controls.
  • Firebase: information processed by Firebase is retained according to the Firebase products used, our configuration and Google's retention policies.
  • AdMob: advertising information is handled and retained by Google and its advertising partners under their applicable policies.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we keep your message only as long as reasonably needed to respond and handle the request. You can ask us to delete correspondence we control.

To request deletion of information that we control, email us using the contact details in section 14. Information held independently by CrazyGames or Google may need to be managed or requested directly from those providers. Uninstalling the Android app or clearing browser storage does not necessarily delete information already processed by third-party services.

9. Children's privacy

The game is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. We do not ask players to provide personal information to play. Advertising and age-related settings are subject to the requirements of the platforms, the services used and applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us using the details in section 14 and we will review the request.

10. Security

Information sent to third-party services is handled using those providers' systems and security measures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
