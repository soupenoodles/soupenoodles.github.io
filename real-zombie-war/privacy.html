Privacy Policy

Real Zombie War · by Soupe Noodles · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Real Zombie War ("the game", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by Soupe Noodles ("we", "us"). By installing and playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location, and the game does not have chat or social features.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (for example coins, gems, upgrades, the stage you reached and your settings) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

You can also learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not sell your personal information.
