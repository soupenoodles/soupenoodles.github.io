Corner Shop: Idle TV Window
Privacy Policy

Published by Soupe Noodles · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Corner Shop: Idle TV Window ("the game") is a single-player idle and merge game that runs in your web browser. This page explains what information the game uses and who else may handle it. We wrote it in plain words on purpose.

In short: the game has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location. Your progress is saved on your own device (or with your CrazyGames account if you are signed in there). We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

  • Your real name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Your precise location.
  • Payment details. The game has no in-app purchases.

2. Your game progress

The game saves your coins, TVs, upgrades, goals, settings and similar progress so you can come back to it. Where this is stored depends on where you play:

  • On your device: in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device. Clearing your browser data, or using the "Reset game" button in the settings menu, deletes it.
  • On CrazyGames: the game can use the CrazyGames data module. For guests, this data stays in the browser. If you are signed in to CrazyGames, CrazyGames can store it with your account so it follows you across devices. This is handled by CrazyGames (see section 3).

The game also stores a few small settings in local storage, such as sound, screen shake and similar options. A built-in test log used while we test new versions stays in your browser. We only see it if you choose to copy it and send it to us.

4. Advertising

The game itself does not show ads at this time. When you play on CrazyGames, the platform may show ads around the game under its own rules. We do not control which ads are shown or what data the ad providers use. If we add ads to the game later, we will update this page first.

5. How we use information

  • To save and load your progress.
  • To run and improve the game, for example by fixing bugs reported to us.
  • To reply when you contact us.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Information is handled only by the services listed in section 3, under their own policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: you can delete it at any time with the "Reset game" button in the settings menu, or by clearing your browser data.
  • Data held by CrazyGames, Google or Discord: we cannot delete this for you. Please ask those services directly.
  • If you wrote to us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it. You can ask us to delete it at support@soupenoodles.com.

8. Children's privacy

The game is made for a general audience, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not ask for any personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your save data stays on your device or with the platform you play on, and we do not run our own server for it. No method of storage or transfer over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as the right to ask what is held about you, to correct it or to delete it. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, most of these requests concern the services in section 3. You can contact us and we will help point you to the right place.
