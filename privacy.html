Privacy Policy

Real Zombie War: Squad Shooter · Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information Real Zombie War: Squad Shooter (“the game”), made by Soupe Noodles (“we”, “us”), collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Contact: soupenoodle54@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: coins, gems, upgrades, levels, hats and skins, settings, your best scores and the name you choose for the leaderboard. This stays on your device unless described below.

Each service has its own privacy policy: Unity, Google (how Google uses data from apps), Meta, Apple.

3. Ads and your consent

The game is free and shows ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › AD PRIVACY. On iPhone, ads are only personalised if you allow tracking when asked; you can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Without consent, the game shows non-personalised ads.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. How long we keep information

Information on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the game. Leaderboard data is kept while the game’s leaderboard exists, or until you delete it as described below. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

6. Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings › DELETE MY DATA. This deletes your progress on the device, your online player account with your leaderboard name and scores, and resets your ad consent. You can also email us at soupenoodle54@gmail.com to ask for deletion. Data held by Google Play Games, Google or Meta can be managed in your Google or Meta account settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at soupenoodle54@gmail.com to use these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Information sent online is sent over encrypted connections. No method is completely secure, but we use the protections provided by the services above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be shown in the game or on the store page.
