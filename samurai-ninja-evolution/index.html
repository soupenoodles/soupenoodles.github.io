Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to play on CrazyGames. Optional videos give you short boosts like 2x strength.

Where can I play?

In any modern browser on CrazyGames, on computer, phone or tablet. An Android version is coming soon to Google Play.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically. Log in to CrazyGames to keep your progress across devices.

You can see up to 16 players in the same world, with their names, ranks, swords and pets. Fights and progress stay your own, so nobody can ruin your run.
